THE FIRE BEHIND THE FLAME

Revived. Reformed.
Aligned.

HOFIRE was birthed in 2020 out of a deep hunger and thirst to see God move in the lives of young people and in our generation. What began as a small gathering of young people from one church has grown into a vision for young people across churches and communities to encounter God, grow in faith, discover their God-given purpose and live according to His calling.

As a vision grew, we began to look beyond one church and envision a platform where young people from different churches and communities could encounter God, discover purpose and live according to His calling. Our desire is simply to see a generation that knows God, is formed by Scripture, and lives with courage in this world.
