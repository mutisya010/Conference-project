HOFIRE is a Christian youth movement that began in 2020 from a deep hunger and thirst to see God move in the lives of young people and in our generation.

What began as a gathering of young people from one church has grown into a vision for young people across churches and communities to encounter God, grow in faith, discover their God-given purpose and live according to His Word.
